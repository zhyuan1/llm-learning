A cinematic ultra-wide 2.35:1 conceptual cover image for a Chinese tech article titled "像训练神经网络一样训练 Skill" (
Training Skills Like Training Neural Networks).

**Core Visual Concept:**
A glowing Markdown document (SKILL.md) floats at the center, rendered as a luminous white card with faint text lines
visible. Around it, neural network nodes and connection weights radiate outward as electric-blue light tendrils — the
document IS the parameter, the network IS the document. A training loop arrow (circular gradient arc) wraps around the
card, suggesting iteration and optimization.

**Background:**
Deep dark navy-black (#0a0e1a), subtle grid lines fading toward edges, creating depth. Faint hexagonal neural-net
texture in the background at very low opacity.

**Lighting & Glow:**
Electric cyan (#22d3ee) and violet (#a78bfa) light emanate from the central document card. Soft bloom effect. The
training loop arc glows cyan-to-violet gradient.

**Central Element:**

- A glowing white document card (SKILL.md) slightly tilted, with faint code/markdown lines
- Neural weight nodes (small bright circles) floating around it, connected by thin glowing lines
- Circular arrow loop around the card in gradient cyan-violet, representing the training cycle
