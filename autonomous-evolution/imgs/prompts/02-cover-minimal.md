# Cover Prompt: 自主进化（极简版）

A minimal flat-vector cover. Dark background #0d1117. Wide cinematic 2.35:1 ratio.

## Visual Concept

Single visual element centered slightly left: one thin glowing circle (teal/cyan, #00e5cc), clean stroke, no fill.
Inside the circle, a single small arrow following the ring — suggesting a loop. That's all. No other shapes, no
patterns, no decorations.

Generous whitespace surrounding the circle on all sides.

Very subtle: faint concentric ring echo (same teal, 10% opacity) slightly larger than the main ring, suggesting "outer
loop" without visual noise.

## Typography

Right side of canvas, vertically centered:

- Title: "自主进化" — large, bold, clean sans-serif, white, #ffffff
- Subtitle below in smaller size, gray #888888: "当 AI 开始为自己写代码"

## Constraints

- NO multiple rings, NO overlapping circles, NO neon rainbow colors
- NO labels on the ring
- NO circuit board texture
- NO gradients on the ring — single solid teal color only
- Maximum simplicity: one ring, one arrow, two lines of text, dark background
