# Cover Image Prompt

A clean, modern flat-vector cover image for a technical deep-dive article about AI Agent Harness architecture.

## Visual Concept

The central metaphor is a "harness as operating system" — an elegant layered system diagram rendered as flat vector
illustration. The composition shows a stylized circular execution loop (representing the ReAct
thought-action-observation cycle) at the center, surrounded by concentric rings representing the 12 harness components.
Thin connecting lines radiate outward like circuit traces.

## Layout

COMPOSITION:

- Background: deep navy blue gradient, dark and cool, technical feel
- CENTER (40% of canvas): A glowing circular loop diagram — three nodes connected in a triangle cycle, each node is a
  solid circle with a single white icon: Thought (brain icon), Action (gear icon), Observation (eye icon). Connected by
  curved arrows forming a loop. Rendered with soft blue-white glow around the circle. The center area inside the
  triangle is EMPTY — no text, no labels, no numbers, no color codes
- SURROUNDING RING: 12 small hexagonal nodes arranged evenly in a ring around the center loop. Each hexagon contains
  only a simple white icon (no text labels, no Chinese characters on the nodes). Connected to center by thin radiating
  lines
- LEFT SIDE: Subtle grid or circuit pattern in the background at very low opacity
- TOP-LEFT: Small minimal "stack" illustration — 5 thin horizontal bars of decreasing length, pure decorative, no text
  labels

## Typography

NO TEXT in the image at all. No title, no subtitle, no labels anywhere. Pure visual only.

## Color Palette

- Background: very dark navy blue, almost black with blue tint
- Primary accent: medium tech blue for main diagram elements and glow
- Secondary accent: light sky blue for subtitle text and ring node borders
- All diagram lines and icons: crisp white
- Subtle background elements: dark steel blue, barely visible
- Glow effect: soft blue-white radial light behind central loop

## Style

Flat vector, no gradients on shapes (only background gradient allowed). Clean geometric forms. Circuit-board aesthetic
without being literal. Professional, cool-toned, technical. 40% whitespace maintained. No realistic humans or complex
illustrations. Crisp edges, precise alignment.

## Mood

Balanced — authoritative and technical, but approachable. Like a high-quality technical conference talk slide, not a
startup pitch deck.

## Aspect Ratio

2.35:1 ultra-wide cinematic format
