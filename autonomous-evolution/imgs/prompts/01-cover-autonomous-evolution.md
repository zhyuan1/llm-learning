# Cover Prompt: 自主进化

A flat-vector conceptual cover illustration. Dark background (#0d1117 deep navy black). Aspect ratio 2.35:1 wide
cinematic.

## Visual Concept

Four concentric rings / loops arranged concentrically, glowing with different colors on dark background:

- Innermost ring: soft purple, labeled concept "执行"
- Second ring: teal/cyan, labeled concept "验证"
- Third ring: blue, labeled concept "触发"
- Outermost ring: orange-red gradient, labeled concept "进化"

Each ring has a small arrow indicating rotation/iteration direction. The outermost ring has a special inward-pointing
arrow that loops back toward the center, suggesting the outer loop improves the inner ones.

In the center of all rings: a small glowing white circuit/code node symbol, representing the AI agent core.

Subtle background: faint grid lines or circuit board texture at very low opacity, dark navy.

## Typography

Title text: "自主进化" in large clean sans-serif bold white font, positioned lower-right or lower-center area, not
overlapping the rings.
Subtitle in smaller gray text below: "当 AI 开始为自己写代码"

## Style Requirements

- Flat vector illustration style, NO photorealism, NO 3D renders
- Dark background: #0d1117 or similar deep navy/black
- Ring colors: glowing neon-style thin strokes, no fills, just colored outlines with subtle glow
- Clean geometric shapes only
- Color palette: purple, teal, blue, orange-red for four rings; white for text; gray for subtitle
- Mood: balanced — professional and forward-looking, not aggressive
- No human figures, no robots, no clipart
- Breathing room: substantial whitespace around the rings
- Cinematic wide format feel (2.35:1)
